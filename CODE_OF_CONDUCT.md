# Code of Conduct

MCP Portico is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and
constructive community for everyone who participates in the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and receive technical feedback professionally.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, documentation, and decisions rather than
  people.
- Protect private information and do not publish credentials, vulnerability
  details, or personal data.
- Follow the project's security policy when reporting security issues.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, intimidation, doxxing,
sexualized behavior, deliberate disruption, and publishing someone else's
private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through the
[repository owner profile](https://github.com/yashcodelabs). Do not create a
public issue for an allegation or include sensitive personal information in a
public thread.
Maintainers may remove content, close discussions, restrict participation, or
take other action they consider appropriate. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as practical.

This code of conduct is adapted from the principles of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
